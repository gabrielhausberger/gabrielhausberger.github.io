---
title: Norwegian Wood by Haruki Murakami
---

One of my favorite books.

I started this book because of its connection to memory, different phases in life, time, and change.

Each scene is drawn clearly in my head and Murakami does an awesome work in setting the atmosphere. After each reading session, my mind would be heavy for some hours, not only processing the story but also reflecting about my own memories. 

Despite how depraved or vulgar one might consider, there is something about Watanabe's relation to women that appears interesting to me. I do not appreciate the self-freedom of which he has sex with different women, even more when considering he had someone he considered a girlfriend. But the appreciation for the different roles each of them had in his life is peculiar. I could relate to how his relation to Midori and his relation with Naoko was so distant that they seemed to belong to different lives. It justifies part of Watanabe's confusion and indecision. Even Nagasawa had such strong contrast with other parts of his life, that I see how he would end up feeling detached from the multiples hookups.
